Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here’s exactly what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Last updated: April 2025

1. Who We Are

Flint Apron Pantry (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website flintapronpantry.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our site or interact with us.

We are an independent recipe journal based in the Pacific Northwest, USA. You can reach us anytime at hello@flintapronpantry.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

When you contact us through our contact form, we collect the following:

  • Name — so we know how to address you.
  • Email address — so we can reply.
  • Subject — to route your message correctly.
  • Message content — whatever you choose to share with us.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may collect basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and the time and date of your visit. This information is aggregated and used to understand how people use the site so we can improve it. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only use it to respond to you and to keep the site running smoothly.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, questions, and corrections.
  • To improve our website, recipes, and content based on how readers use the site.
  • To send occasional updates if you have opted in (we don’t have a newsletter yet, but if we do, it will be opt-in only).
  • To comply with legal obligations, if any apply.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use minimal cookies — only those necessary for the site to function and to understand basic traffic patterns. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Please see our Cookies Policy for more details.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you share with us. Emails and contact form submissions are stored securely, and access is limited to the site owner. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We may use trusted third-party tools (like email hosting) to help operate the site. These providers only access your data to perform services on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose it.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or in response to valid legal requests, we may disclose your information.

We never sell your data. Ever.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access the information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of your information.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing.

To exercise any of these rights, just email us at hello@flintapronpantry.com. We’ll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to other websites (for example, ingredient sources or related articles). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

Flint Apron Pantry is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please reach out:

Email: hello@flintapronpantry.com

Based in: Pacific Northwest, USA
