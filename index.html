Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need special equipment to start?

Not at all. A cast iron skillet, a Dutch oven, and a few glass jars will get you through 90% of our recipes. We're firm believers that good cooking is about technique, not tools.

Are your recipes tested?

Every single one. We test each recipe at least twice — once in our home kitchen and once in a borrowed kitchen with different equipment — before it's published.

Can I use your recipes in my own blog?

We're happy to be inspired by each other, but please don't copy our recipes verbatim. Rewrite in your own words, credit us with a link, and share the love.

How do you choose which recipes to feature?

We look for dishes that teach something — a technique, a ratio, a principle. If a recipe only works once, it's not for us. We want recipes you'll cook for years.

Do you accept guest posts?

Rarely, and only from cooks we've followed for a while. If you have a story about food that feels like it belongs here, send us a note via the contact page.

What does "Flint Apron" mean?

It's a nod to the flint stones used to start fires — and the aprons our grandmothers wore while tending those fires. It's about tradition, patience, and the spark of a good meal.

About Flint Apron Pantry

Flint Apron Pantry began in a small kitchen in the Pacific Northwest, where the rain kept us indoors and the oven kept us warm. We're a reader-supported publication — no ads, no sponsored content, just recipes and stories that we believe in.

Our editorial approach is simple: we cook, we write, we test again. Every recipe on this site has been made in a home kitchen, with ingredients you can find at a well-stocked grocery store or farmers market.

Contact

We love hearing from readers. Whether you have a question about a recipe, a correction, or just want to say hello, you can reach us at hello@flintapronpantry.com. We read every email, though it may take us a few days to respond.

Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Flint Apron Pantry collects only the information you voluntarily provide — your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter, and any messages you send us directly. We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Our site uses basic analytics to understand which articles are resonating with readers. This data is anonymized and aggregated. We do not use tracking pixels, and we do not serve targeted advertising.
