Plain-language terms covering content, privacy, and your use of Flint Apron Pantry.

Short version: We respect your privacy, we don't sell your data, and we ask that you respect our content. Cook, read, enjoy — and if you share a recipe, link back to us.

All recipes, articles, photographs, illustrations, and other content on Flint Apron Pantry are owned by us or our contributors and are protected by copyright. You are welcome to:

  • Cook our recipes for personal, non-commercial use.
  • Print or save recipes for your own kitchen use.

You may not republish full recipes or articles, use our content for AI training without permission, or sell our content. If you'd like to feature our work, please reach out — we're often happy to collaborate.

2. Privacy & Data Collection

We collect very little data, and we never sell it. Here's what we do collect and why:

Information you give us

If you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us via email, we collect your name and email address. We use this only to send you updates or reply to your message. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use basic analytics (such as privacy-friendly, aggregate stats) to understand which recipes are popular. This data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally. We do not use invasive tracking or sell any data to third parties.

Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies for site functionality and anonymous analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more detail, see our Cookies Policy.

Depending on your location (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Ask us anything about how your data is handled.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We'll respond within 30 days.

Cooking involves inherent risks (sharp knives, hot surfaces, allergens). While we test our recipes carefully, you are responsible for your own safety and for checking ingredients for allergens. See our full Disclaimer for details.

We may update these Terms of Privacy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we do, we'll update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the new terms.

Questions about these terms or your privacy? Send us a note. We're a small team and we read every message.
