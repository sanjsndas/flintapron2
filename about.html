About Flint Apron Pantry

Slow-cooked stories, heirloom techniques, and practical kitchen wisdom — for cooks who read.

Flint Apron Pantry began in a small kitchen in the Pacific Northwest, where the rain kept us indoors and the oven kept us warm. What started as a personal collection of family recipes — scribbled on index cards and stained with butter — grew into a journal for anyone who believes that cooking is more than just following instructions.

We are a reader-supported publication. No ads, no sponsored content, no hidden agendas. Just recipes and stories we believe in, tested in home kitchens and written for people who want to understand the why behind the how.

Our Editorial Approach

Every recipe on this site has been made in a home kitchen, with ingredients you can find at a well-stocked grocery store or farmers market. We test each recipe at least twice — once in our own kitchen and once in a borrowed kitchen with different equipment — before it's published.

We look for dishes that teach something: a technique, a ratio, a principle. If a recipe only works once, it's not for us. We want recipes you'll cook for years, the kind that become part of your own family's story.

“We're firm believers that good cooking is about technique, not tools. A cast iron skillet, a Dutch oven, and a few glass jars will get you through 90% of what we make.”

What "Flint Apron" Means

It's a nod to the flint stones used to start fires — and the aprons our grandmothers wore while tending those fires. It's about tradition, patience, and the spark of a good meal. The name reminds us that cooking is an ancient craft, passed down hand to hand, generation to generation.

Meet the Editor

EH

Eliza Hartwell

Editor & Recipe Developer

Eliza has been cooking professionally and writing about food for over a decade. She believes that the best meals are the ones that take time, and that a well-worn apron is a badge of honor. When she's not testing recipes, she's tending her sourdough starter or hunting for ramps in the spring.

Our Promise to You

  • Tested recipes: Every recipe is made at least twice before it's published.
  • No ads, no sponsored content: We're reader-supported and independent.
  • Privacy respected: We never sell or share your information.
  • Real food: Accessible ingredients, honest techniques, and stories worth reading.

Whether you're here to master sourdough, perfect a braise, or simply find something good to cook for dinner, we're glad you're here. Pull up a chair, pour a glass of wine, and let's get cooking.

— The Flint Apron Pantry Team
